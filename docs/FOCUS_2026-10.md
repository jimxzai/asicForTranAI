# Focus — 2026-10-22

## The one bet
**Machine-checked arithmetic guarantees for quantized LLM inference.** Not an inference engine, not a new bit-width, not a platform.

## Who it is for (in order)
1. **Chip and IP teams** shipping INT4/FP4/MXFP4 inference paths, including fixed-weight silicon (pre-tape-out risk is high and a respin is costly).
2. **Regulated edge-AI suppliers** (automotive, avionics, medical) who need assurance evidence as the ML standards land (ED-324 / ARP6983 expected around now; EU AI Act high-risk dates moved to 2027–28).
3. **Funders:** SBIR/STTR (reauthorized to 2031), where "verified low-precision inference" is a fundable research topic.

## The offer (one sentence)
A fixed-scope **kernel assurance review**: for your quantized pack/decode format and dot-product kernel, we deliver SPARK/Lean proofs of overflow-freedom and decode correctness, plus a bit-exact test oracle. Scope and price are to be learned from the calls; do not quote a number before 3 conversations.

## What we cannot claim
The proofs cover models of the Fortran, not the Fortran. Layout, matmul structure, MXFP4 scale handling and NVFP4 are unproved. No accuracy evaluation on a real model. No accelerator or GPU measurement. No customer. The Alire GNATprove is not a certified release.

## Decision rule
- **Continue (commit 12 months):** ≥3 substantive conversations that confirm the pain *and* (one paid pilot, one LOI, or one grant submission in progress).
- **Pivot to consulting:** conversations confirm the pain but nobody will pay for a product; sell the same review as a service and stop building tools.
- **Shelve:** fewer than 3 conversations after 20 attempts, or no recognition of the pain. Keep the repo as a public portfolio and move effort elsewhere.
- Second gate **2027-01-31**: if "continue", the layout is proved, NVFP4 is added, and one real measurement exists on non-CPU hardware.
